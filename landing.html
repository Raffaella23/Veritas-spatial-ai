What is VERITAS?

An intelligent spatial agent that sees, understands, and analyzes 3D environments through the lens of architectural and XR design. VERITAS combines computer vision, AI reasoning, and spatial intelligence to provide real-time feedback on user experience quality.

Key Features

🔍 Vision Captures 3D scenes in real-time
🧠 Intelligence GPT-4o AI analysis & reasoning
📐 Spatial XR-focused architecture analysis
⚡ Real-Time Instant feedback & recommendations

How It Works

1. Explore: Navigate 3D environment with WASD keys

2. Capture: Press E to send frame to AI brain

3. Analyze: AI evaluates space for UX, XR opportunities, accessibility

4. Learn: Get real-time recommendations for spatial optimization
